# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of experience level, background, identity, appearance, disability, ethnicity,
religion, or anything else that makes people different.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code and kind about people. Critique the change, not the contributor.
- Assume good faith, and ask before assuming a mistake was careless.
- Give reproduction steps and concrete evidence in bug reports; "it doesn't work" is not
  actionable, and a failing fixture is.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change. Technical disagreement is welcome; pressure
  after a decision is not.
- Respect privacy. Do not paste other people's code, credentials or customer data into issues.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Discriminatory language or imagery, in any channel, including commit messages and usernames.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sexual attention of any kind.
- Bad-faith participation: deliberately wasting maintainer time, reopening settled decisions, or
  using issues as a soapbox.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, code
comments — and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report problems to **ankur.halder12345@gmail.com**. Reports are handled confidentially, and the
reporter's identity is not shared without their consent.

Responses escalate with severity: a private clarification, a warning, a temporary block from
participating, then a permanent ban. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or issues that
violate this document, and will explain why.

If you believe a decision was unfair, say so in a private message rather than reopening the
thread — it will be reconsidered by the maintainer with fresh eyes.
